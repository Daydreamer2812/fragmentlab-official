Multiple pieces per image are supported. Browser accepts up to 4,096 uploaded/detected pieces; native uses adaptive RAM and pixel budgets (outer archive ceiling 262,144); 128 scanned strips. Intact and missing-edge fragments use the same automatic workflow, with a memory-aware search. Missing material remains transparent; uncertain groups stay separate. No grid, source size or angle needed. Perspective, curled paper and overlapping pieces are not corrected. A complete placement is not a verified match.
