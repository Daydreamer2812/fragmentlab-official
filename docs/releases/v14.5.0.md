# Shardelix 14.5.0 — robust physical document continuity

No OCR, ML, language model, training set, intact reference or ground-truth
ordering enters reconstruction.

## Changes

- Reuse the coarse same-page bidirectional patch predictions. Evaluate
  one globally coherent bounded vertical shift (up to two patch-center
  steps), local shifts in six longitudinal bands, and a source-ink-weighted
  error. Both the direct and predicted continuations use the same phase.
  Missing cut pixels are not generated.
- Weight the residual by observed ink in its source half patch, with a
  nonzero floor for weak/blank evidence. Normalize by observed and predicted
  energy; compare both directions. The band score uses observed seam energy.
- Apply the three extra residuals only to tall, variable-width, sufficiently
  resolved scan strips, not equal-width, low-resolution, or Fast inputs.
  Keep the calibrated gap bank. If a calibrated gap wins, coarse-residual
  variants cannot replace it. Otherwise select only an alternative
  improving the reciprocal-rank/margin score by at least 0.03. This
  heuristic is not a probability of correct reconstruction.
- Admit a local rim expansion when at least 80% of rows agree with the
  dominant width, the strip is very elongated, all widths are within
  guarded bounds, and the mask still passes the interior-hole criterion.
- No additional nearest-neighbor search is performed, no change to
  rectified output resolution, and no changes to color/irregular or
  digital image routes.

The first six new pages were initially tested against the frozen robust
residual policy (67/129). After a regression on development D2/D001,
the calibrated-gap guard was added; the two affected cases were rerun.
The final three new pages were evaluated only after fixing that rule.
The final held-out group does **not** show an accuracy gain; one case
regresses by one join. The selected sample is not a representative
estimate of general performance across all published data.

Highlights:
- D2/D010: formerly rejected, now 25/25 joins and 26/26 positions.
- D3/D035: 17/20 to 20/20 joins, 4/21 to 21/21 positions.
- D3/D045: 19/23 to 23/23 joins, 0/24 to 24/24 positions.
- D3/D072: 12/20 to 19/20 joins on a new page.
- D2/D020: 19/22 to 16/22, a real regression.
- D3/D012: still 1/22, sparse ink causes color-strip routing.
- D3/D018: still 2/23, difficult for all evaluated residuals.

On the three original real DocDataset pages, all 119/119 directed joins
and 122/122 positions remain correct. Eight historical synthetic damage
stress cases retain the same 73/248 undirected joins as V14.4.
Matching-only timing is recorded, but concurrent work on a shared CPU
prevents a controlled V14.5 speedup claim. The additional residuals reuse
existing nearest-neighbor predictions, not extra patch-tree searches.

baseline, first-pass, final and protected-gap retest results. See [dataset
protocol](../DATASETS.md) for download and reproduction. The source
