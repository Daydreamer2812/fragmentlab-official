# Data and asset provenance

Only small, shareable generated example fixtures are stored under
`data/examples/`.

| Asset | Origin |
|:--|:--|
| [straight_texture.png](examples/straight_texture.png) | Deterministic synthetic RGB noise; [generation parameters](examples/straight_texture.README.md) |
| [text_document.png](examples/text_document.png) | Generated by [create_document_fixture.py](../scripts/create_document_fixture.py) |

The [photographic showcase](../docs/assets/shardelix-photo-showcase.webp)
uses the NASA astronaut photograph distributed through scikit-image, as
identified by its [generation script](../scripts/make_photo_showcase.py)
and [recorded provenance](../reports/v13/photo-showcase.json).
The [earlier showcase](../docs/assets/reconstruction-showcase.webp) is a
historical generated experiment; see [V12 results](../docs/V12_RESULTS.md).

External datasets (DocDataset, mechanical document datasets, DAFNE and
others) are not bundled. Obtain them from the original authors and observe
the applicable terms in the [dataset documentation](../docs/DATASETS.md).
The [MIT License](../LICENSE) for Shardelix does not grant rights to third-party
datasets, dependencies or imagery.

Use ignored directories for local data and results:

- `data/external/`: downloaded datasets
- `data/raw/`: raw local inputs
- `data/synthetic/`: generated fixtures
- `outputs/`: reconstruction results and experiment logs

Never commit private scan contents, original benchmark datasets, credentials
or generated result archives.
